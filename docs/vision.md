# BEACN Vision

## Our Mission

To make understanding infrastructure simple.

## Our Vision

Modern infrastructure is increasingly distributed, dynamic and complex. Understanding what exists, how it is connected and what state it is in should not require expensive enterprise tooling or fragmented solutions.

BEACN exists to provide a simple, open and extensible platform for discovering, inventorying and observing infrastructure.

Our goal is not to replace every monitoring platform. Instead, BEACN aims to become the trusted source of truth for infrastructure discovery and inventory, providing a solid foundation that can integrate with existing monitoring, automation and management tools.

## Principles

BEACN is built around a small number of core principles:

- Simplicity before complexity.
- Observations over assumptions.
- Open by default.
- Automation where it adds value.
- Extensibility without unnecessary complexity.
- Documentation as a first-class feature.

## Long-Term Goals

As BEACN evolves, we aim to provide:

- Cross-platform device discovery
- Rich infrastructure inventory
- Optional agent-based telemetry
- Plugin architecture
- Cloud integrations
- API-first automation
- Historical observations
- Infrastructure visualisation

Every new feature should support the mission:

> Make understanding infrastructure simple.
